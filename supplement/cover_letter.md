Dear Editor,

We submit "Calibration-Aware Evaluation of Detector-Assisted Traffic Control in Edge-IIoT Networks" to the Journal of Network and Computer Applications.

The paper addresses detector-assisted traffic control as a network application problem rather than a detector-only classification task. It proposes a leakage-safe and calibration-aware evaluation framework, instantiates a transparent calibrated reference controller (CARA-TC), and compares it with validation-selected learned controllers under artifact, temporal, cross-dataset, and OVS executability diagnostics.

We believe the manuscript fits JNCA because it connects network-security detection outputs to deployable traffic-control actions and evaluates the resulting service-security trade-off under realistic network-application constraints. A reproducibility artifact and supplementary evidence are provided for review. The manuscript and artifact are not anonymized because JNCA uses single-blind review.

Sincerely,

The Authors
